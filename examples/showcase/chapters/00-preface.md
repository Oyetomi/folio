Every system that serves requests has a queue in it somewhere, whether or not anyone drew it on a diagram. This short book covers the two results that explain most of what you will see in practice, then shows how to check them with a simulation.

It is also an example. The source is a handful of Markdown files in the Folio repository, and this PDF is what `folio build` makes from them.
